Day 2 of 90 with @MyFirstHack. I expected a technical lesson. Instead, I got one of the most honest lessons I've ever had about learning itself.

The lesson broke down the four traps that make most people quit cybersecurity learning: overwhelm, tutorial hell, boredom, and life getting in the way. Not because it's too hard, but because they set themselves up wrong.

I recognised myself in one of them immediately: overwhelm.

Cybersecurity is huge. Networking, Linux, cryptography, web security, malware, hundreds of tools. Trying to learn all of it at once is the fastest way to burn out.

The line that's going to stay with me: Master one fundamental at a time (like the CIA Triad: confidentiality, integrity, availability) before chasing advanced tools.

My plan: stick to the fundamentals first, practise what I learn instead of just watching tutorials, and keep it to 30 minutes a day.

Consistency beats intensity. Every time. For 90 days.
